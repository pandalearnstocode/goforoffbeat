Holiday

Northeast India · leave-stretch

Meghalaya, under the clouds

Waterfalls, living root bridges and highland villages in a spring route with enough days for the drive between stops.

Places
Shillong · Cherrapunji · Mawlynnong
Dates
20–28 March 2027 · 9 days
Holiday
Holi 23 Mar + Good Friday 26 Mar
  • Best for: active travellers who can take 2–3 weekdays off
  • Local taste: jadoh, tungrymbai and seasonal Khasi dishes
View the trip outline
  • Start and finish in Guwahati, with a private transfer to Shillong.
  • Stay two nights near Cherrapunji for falls, caves and the living root bridge walk.
  • Use the remaining days for Shillong and a village stay, weather permitting.

Holi is an optional/restricted holiday for some workplaces and varies by state. The nine-day window assumes leave on 24–25 March, and possibly 23 March where Holi is not a holiday.
