Go when a place is most itself.

When to go, where to go

October · Bengal

Kolkata during Durga Puja

For pandal art, evening walks and the city at full volume, plan around the Puja dates. The days are busy and accommodation fills early, so choose a base with easy transport and keep the itinerary light.

Look for: community bhog, khichuri, labra, luchi and seasonal sweets. Food menus vary by pandal and neighborhood.

November–February · North Bengal

Dooars, Darjeeling & Kalimpong

Cooler, generally drier months suit forest-edge stays and clear hill days. In the Dooars, park access and safaris follow seasonal opening and permit rules; in the hills, leave buffer time for winding roads.

Look for: Darjeeling tea, momos and warming bowls of thukpa. Cooch Behar’s Ras Mela is usually held around November–December; confirm the annual dates before travelling.

December–January · Bengal

Winter in Kolkata & Santiniketan

Comfortable days make this a lovely time for city neighborhoods and Birbhum’s winter fair season. Poush Mela brings music, crafts and a lively Santiniketan campus; dates and venue arrangements are announced locally.

Look for: pithe, patishapta and sweets made with winter nolen gur, plus Kolkata’s winter koraishutir kochuri.

January–March · Sikkim

Himalayan winter festivals

Choose a low-altitude route for crisp mountain days and village culture. Losar, Losoong, Maghey Sankranti and Gutor Cham follow local calendars and are not all held on the same date each year.

Look for: momos, thukpa and phagshapa. Ask your host about community events before planning around a monastery festival.

October–November · Meghalaya

Garo Hills for Wangala

Wangala is a Garo harvest festival, with the largest 100 Drums gathering usually in the first week of November. Event dates and venues vary; visit with a local guide and follow community guidance around ceremonies.

Look for: Khasi jadoh and tungrymbai, and Garo dishes at community food stalls. Ask about ingredients and dietary needs in advance.

September · Kerala

Onam in the backwaters

Onam is a harvest celebration with cultural events and boat races in different parts of Kerala. The 2027 Onam date is expected on 12 September; local programs vary and the season is busy, so plan stays early.

Look for: an Onam sadya, a multi-dish feast served on a banana leaf. Confirm feast and event bookings with local hosts.

October–March · Rajasthan

Fort towns in cooler weather

Cooler months are comfortable for Jaipur, Jodhpur and desert routes. Pushkar Fair is held in October or November, but its dates shift each year; plan around the published event schedule.

Look for: dal baati churma, pyaaz kachori and ghevar. Festival market food and opening hours can change during peak days.

November–February · Sundarbans

Waterways in the cooler season

Cooler months are a comfortable starting point for a boat-led visit. Build a route around official entry points, park permissions and the tide; wildlife sightings can never be promised.

Look for: fresh local fish and home-style Bengali cooking at your stay. Ask about seasonal menus and responsible sourcing.

March–April · Meghalaya

Spring walks in the hills

Spring can be a good shoulder-season window for village walks and waterfalls before the heavier monsoon. Trails may still be wet, so check local conditions and choose a guide who knows the route.

Look for: jadoh with dohkhleh and tungrymbai, plus rice cakes such as pu khlein. Vegetarian options are available; ask hosts ahead.

Travel with care: A festival is a living community event, not a staged attraction. Dates, public access and food availability change year to year. We’ll check local schedules when planning, and we recommend asking before photographing people or rituals.
Useful before you lock dates

Seasonal planning notes

Keep a weather buffer

Monsoon rain can affect roads, trails and boat schedules. We build in flexible transfers where conditions call for it.

Book festival stays early

Durga Puja, Onam and major fair weeks bring high demand. Decide what you want to see and reserve a convenient base.

Confirm the local calendar

Regional holidays and festival dates may differ from central lists. Confirm with your employer and local organizers before booking.
